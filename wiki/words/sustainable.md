## 意思

**adj.** 可持續的、能永續的：able to continue without damaging the environment

## 例句

> Governments should invest in sustainable forms of transport.

## 不同用法

### 1. 形容詞修飾名詞

> Many companies now focus on sustainable packaging.

放在名詞前，表示對環境友善。

### 2. 表示「能長期維持的」（非環境）

> Working twelve hours a day is not sustainable.

也可指生活方式、經濟成長能否持續。

### 3. 名詞 sustainability

> Sustainability has become a key goal for many cities.

抽象名詞，常當主詞。
