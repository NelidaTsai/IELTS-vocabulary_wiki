## 意思

**n.** 隱私：the state of being free from public attention

## 例句

> Social media users often underestimate the risks to their privacy.

## 不同用法

### 1. 不可數名詞

> Everyone has the right to privacy.

the right to privacy 是固定說法。

### 2. invade / protect someone's privacy

> Reporters should not invade the privacy of ordinary people.

常搭配 invade、protect、respect。

### 3. 放在名詞前當修飾語

> Users should check the privacy settings on their accounts.

privacy settings／policy／concerns。
