## 意思

**n.** 創新：a new idea, method or product

## 例句

> Technological innovation has transformed the way people work.

## 不同用法

### 1. 不可數：創新這件事

> Innovation is the key to long-term economic growth.

抽象概念，不加 a。

### 2. 可數：一項新發明

> The smartphone was one of the greatest innovations of the century.

指具體的新產品或方法。

### 3. 形容詞 innovative

> The company is known for its innovative designs.

描述有創意的。
