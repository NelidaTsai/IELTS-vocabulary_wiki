## 意思

**v.** 減輕、緩和：to make something harmful less severe

## 例句

> Planting trees can help mitigate the effects of urban heat.

## 不同用法

### 1. 及物：mitigate + 負面名詞

> Early warning systems can mitigate the damage caused by floods.

受詞通常是傷害、風險、影響。

### 2. 被動語態

> The risks can be mitigated through careful planning.

寫作中常用被動，語氣較客觀。

### 3. 名詞 mitigation

> Climate change mitigation requires international cooperation.

常見片語 climate change mitigation。
