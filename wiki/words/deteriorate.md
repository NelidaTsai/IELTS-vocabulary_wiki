## 意思

**v.** 惡化、變差：to become worse over time

## 例句

> Air quality in many cities has deteriorated over the past decade.

## 不同用法

### 1. 不及物：主詞 + deteriorate

> The patient's condition deteriorated overnight.

不接受詞，主詞本身「變差」。

### 2. deteriorate into + 名詞

> The peaceful protest deteriorated into violence.

惡化成某種更糟的狀態。

### 3. 名詞 deterioration of + 名詞

> The deterioration of public infrastructure worries many residents.

寫作中常改用名詞讓句子更正式。
