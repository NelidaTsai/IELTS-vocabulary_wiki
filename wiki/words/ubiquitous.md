## 意思

**adj.** 無所不在的：seeming to be everywhere

## 例句

> Smartphones have become ubiquitous in modern society.

## 不同用法

### 1. be ubiquitous in + 地方

> Coffee shops are ubiquitous in big cities.

描述某東西到處都是。

### 2. 放在名詞前

> The ubiquitous use of social media affects teenagers' sleep.

正式寫作常用來取代 very common。

### 3. 名詞 ubiquity

> The ubiquity of mobile phones has changed how we communicate.

抽象名詞，適合當主詞。
