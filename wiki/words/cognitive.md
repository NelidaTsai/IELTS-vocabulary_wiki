## 意思

**adj.** 認知的：relating to thinking, learning and understanding

## 例句

> Learning a second language may improve children's cognitive development.

## 不同用法

### 1. 修飾能力或發展

> Regular exercise may improve cognitive function in older adults.

cognitive function／ability。

### 2. cognitive load

> Long instructions increase the cognitive load on learners.

指大腦處理資訊的負擔。

### 3. 名詞 cognition

> Sleep plays an important role in human cognition.

學術文章常用。
