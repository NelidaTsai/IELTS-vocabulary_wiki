## 意思

**n.** 學費；教學：money paid for teaching, or the teaching itself

## 例句

> Rising tuition fees prevent some students from going to university.

## 不同用法

### 1. 學費：tuition fees

> Many students take part-time jobs to pay their tuition fees.

英式寫法常用 tuition fees。

### 2. 教學、家教：private tuition

> Some parents spend a lot on private tuition for their children.

不可數，指授課本身。

### 3. free tuition

> Several countries offer free tuition at public universities.

討論教育政策時常見。
