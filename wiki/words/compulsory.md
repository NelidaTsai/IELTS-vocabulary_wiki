## 意思

**adj.** 義務的、強制的：required by law or a rule

## 例句

> Physical education is compulsory in most secondary schools.

## 不同用法

### 1. 放在名詞前

> Compulsory military service exists in several countries.

修飾某項必須做的事。

### 2. be compulsory for + 人

> Wearing a helmet is compulsory for all cyclists.

說明對誰強制。

### 3. make something compulsory

> Some argue that voting should be made compulsory.

議論文常見句型。
