## 意思

**n.** 讀寫能力：the ability to read and write

## 例句

> Improving adult literacy can reduce unemployment.

## 不同用法

### 1. 讀寫能力

> The government launched a campaign to raise literacy levels.

基本意思。

### 2. 某領域的能力：digital / financial literacy

> Financial literacy should be taught at school.

前面加領域，表示該方面的知識。

### 3. 形容詞 literate / illiterate

> Being computer literate is essential in most jobs today.

computer literate 是「會用電腦的」。
