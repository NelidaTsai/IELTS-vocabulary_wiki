## 單字一覽

| 單字 | 詞性 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| [deteriorate](../words/deteriorate.md) | v. | 惡化、變差 |
| [sustainable](../words/sustainable.md) | adj. | 可持續的、能永續的 |
| [emission](../words/emission.md) | n. | 排放、排放物 |
| [mitigate](../words/mitigate.md) | v. | 減輕、緩和 |
| [biodiversity](../words/biodiversity.md) | n. | 生物多樣性 |
| [conservation](../words/conservation.md) | n. | 保育、保護 |

## 依用途分組

- **描述問題**：[emission](../words/emission.md)（carbon emissions）、[deteriorate](../words/deteriorate.md)（air quality has deteriorated）、loss of [biodiversity](../words/biodiversity.md)
- **提出解決方式**：[mitigate](../words/mitigate.md) the impact、[sustainable](../words/sustainable.md) development、wildlife [conservation](../words/conservation.md)

## 例句精選

> Reducing carbon emissions is essential to slowing climate change.

> Governments should invest in sustainable forms of transport.
