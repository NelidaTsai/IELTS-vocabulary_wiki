## 定義

搭配詞（collocation）是習慣上固定一起出現的字組。只背單字的意思，寫作時容易組出文法正確、但聽起來不自然的句子；連搭配一起背，用字會更道地。

## 知識庫中的例子

| 搭配 | 單字頁 |
|---|---|
| carbon emissions／cut emissions | [emission](../words/emission.md) |
| mitigate the impact | [mitigate](../words/mitigate.md) |
| compulsory education | [compulsory](../words/compulsory.md) |
| vocational training | [vocational](../words/vocational.md) |
| invasion of privacy | [privacy](../words/privacy.md) |
| become obsolete | [obsolete](../words/obsolete.md) |
