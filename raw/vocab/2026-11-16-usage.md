# 用法補充筆記：18 個單字的不同用法

- 建立日期：2026-11-16
- 說明：補充每個字的不同用法（詞性變化、句型、語意），例句為 Claude 自行撰寫。**待 Nelida 校對。**
- 此檔為原始資料，ingest 後不再修改；要更正請另開新檔。

| word | 用法 | 例句 | 說明 |
|---|---|---|---|
| deteriorate | 不及物：主詞 + deteriorate | The patient's condition deteriorated overnight. | 不接受詞，主詞本身「變差」 |
| deteriorate | deteriorate into + 名詞 | The peaceful protest deteriorated into violence. | 惡化成某種更糟的狀態 |
| deteriorate | 名詞 deterioration of + 名詞 | The deterioration of public infrastructure worries many residents. | 寫作中常改用名詞讓句子更正式 |
| sustainable | 形容詞修飾名詞 | Many companies now focus on sustainable packaging. | 放在名詞前，表示對環境友善 |
| sustainable | 表示「能長期維持的」（非環境） | Working twelve hours a day is not sustainable. | 也可指生活方式、經濟成長能否持續 |
| sustainable | 名詞 sustainability | Sustainability has become a key goal for many cities. | 抽象名詞，常當主詞 |
| emission | 可數，常用複數 | Car emissions are a major source of urban pollution. | 指具體的排放物時多用 emissions |
| emission | emission of + 物質 | The emission of harmful gases must be strictly controlled. | 強調「排放」這個動作 |
| emission | 動詞 emit | Factories emit large amounts of carbon dioxide. | 改寫時可把名詞換成動詞 |
| mitigate | 及物：mitigate + 負面名詞 | Early warning systems can mitigate the damage caused by floods. | 受詞通常是傷害、風險、影響 |
| mitigate | 被動語態 | The risks can be mitigated through careful planning. | 寫作中常用被動，語氣較客觀 |
| mitigate | 名詞 mitigation | Climate change mitigation requires international cooperation. | 常見片語 climate change mitigation |
| biodiversity | 不可數名詞 | The rainforest is home to remarkable biodiversity. | 不加 a、不用複數 |
| biodiversity | 當主詞描述變化 | Biodiversity is declining at an alarming rate. | 常搭配 decline、loss |
| biodiversity | 形容詞 biodiverse | Coral reefs are among the most biodiverse ecosystems. | 描述某地區物種豐富 |
| conservation | 不可數名詞 | Conservation of natural habitats should be a priority. | 指保護自然、野生動物 |
| conservation | 指「節約」資源 | Water conservation is essential during dry seasons. | energy／water conservation 是節約 |
| conservation | 動詞 conserve | We can conserve energy by switching off unused lights. | 動詞常表示節省、保存 |
| curriculum | 可數，複數 curricula 或 curriculums | Schools in different countries follow very different curricula. | 正式寫作常用 curricula |
| curriculum | on the curriculum | Coding is now on the curriculum in many primary schools. | 「被列入課程」的固定說法 |
| curriculum | 形容詞 curricular / extracurricular | Students are encouraged to join extracurricular activities. | extracurricular 是「課外的」 |
| compulsory | 放在名詞前 | Compulsory military service exists in several countries. | 修飾某項必須做的事 |
| compulsory | be compulsory for + 人 | Wearing a helmet is compulsory for all cyclists. | 說明對誰強制 |
| compulsory | make something compulsory | Some argue that voting should be made compulsory. | 議論文常見句型 |
| literacy | 讀寫能力 | The government launched a campaign to raise literacy levels. | 基本意思 |
| literacy | 某領域的能力：digital / financial literacy | Financial literacy should be taught at school. | 前面加領域，表示該方面的知識 |
| literacy | 形容詞 literate / illiterate | Being computer literate is essential in most jobs today. | computer literate 是「會用電腦的」 |
| vocational | 放在名詞前 | She enrolled in a vocational course in nursing. | 修飾課程、學校、技能 |
| vocational | 與學術教育對比 | Vocational education is as valuable as academic study. | 議論文常與 academic 對照 |
| vocational | 名詞 vocation | He found his true vocation in teaching. | vocation 有「天職、使命感」的意思 |
| tuition | 學費：tuition fees | Many students take part-time jobs to pay their tuition fees. | 英式寫法常用 tuition fees |
| tuition | 教學、家教：private tuition | Some parents spend a lot on private tuition for their children. | 不可數，指授課本身 |
| tuition | free tuition | Several countries offer free tuition at public universities. | 討論教育政策時常見 |
| cognitive | 修飾能力或發展 | Regular exercise may improve cognitive function in older adults. | cognitive function／ability |
| cognitive | cognitive load | Long instructions increase the cognitive load on learners. | 指大腦處理資訊的負擔 |
| cognitive | 名詞 cognition | Sleep plays an important role in human cognition. | 學術文章常用 |
| innovation | 不可數：創新這件事 | Innovation is the key to long-term economic growth. | 抽象概念，不加 a |
| innovation | 可數：一項新發明 | The smartphone was one of the greatest innovations of the century. | 指具體的新產品或方法 |
| innovation | 形容詞 innovative | The company is known for its innovative designs. | 描述有創意的 |
| obsolete | become / render something obsolete | Digital cameras rendered film cameras almost obsolete. | render ... obsolete 是正式寫法 |
| obsolete | 放在名詞前 | Many offices still rely on obsolete software. | 修飾設備、技術、技能 |
| obsolete | 名詞 obsolescence | Planned obsolescence encourages people to buy new products. | planned obsolescence 是「計畫性淘汰」 |
| automation | 不可數名詞 | Automation could replace millions of repetitive jobs. | 指自動化這個趨勢 |
| automation | 動詞 automate | Many banks have automated their customer service. | 及物動詞，受詞是流程或工作 |
| automation | 形容詞 automated | Automated checkouts are becoming common in supermarkets. | 描述自動運作的設備 |
| surveillance | 不可數名詞 | The city has increased surveillance in public spaces. | 不加 a、不用複數 |
| surveillance | under surveillance | The suspect was kept under surveillance for weeks. | 「被監視中」的固定片語 |
| surveillance | 放在名詞前當修飾語 | Surveillance technology is developing rapidly. | surveillance camera／system／technology |
| ubiquitous | be ubiquitous in + 地方 | Coffee shops are ubiquitous in big cities. | 描述某東西到處都是 |
| ubiquitous | 放在名詞前 | The ubiquitous use of social media affects teenagers' sleep. | 正式寫作常用來取代 very common |
| ubiquitous | 名詞 ubiquity | The ubiquity of mobile phones has changed how we communicate. | 抽象名詞，適合當主詞 |
| privacy | 不可數名詞 | Everyone has the right to privacy. | the right to privacy 是固定說法 |
| privacy | invade / protect someone's privacy | Reporters should not invade the privacy of ordinary people. | 常搭配 invade、protect、respect |
| privacy | 放在名詞前當修飾語 | Users should check the privacy settings on their accounts. | privacy settings／policy／concerns |
